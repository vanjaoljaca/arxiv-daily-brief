# What my brief currently looks for

This is not a universal “important papers” list. It is the current taste signal for one personal feed.

## Primary tracks

- education, learning science, AI education, and AI education for children;
- language learning, linguistics, and unusually interesting language research;
- UX, HCI, interaction design, evaluation interfaces, and human–AI collaboration;
- programming, software engineering, testing, developer tools, and design-process tooling;
- AI systems when they reveal a reusable product, interface, or engineering idea.

## Exploratory tracks

- embodiment and somatic experience;
- voice, singing, perception, and motor learning;
- surprising work outside these areas when it teaches a vivid, transferable idea.

These are ranking signals, not hard filters. The brief should not force a weak match just to fill a category, and it should say plainly when the daily batch contains nothing worthwhile for a requested track.

The filter also changes through conversation. A useful connection, an annoyed reaction, or a follow-up question is evidence for future editions—not merely commentary on the current one.
